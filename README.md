# Arcana-Forensics

**Offline-First. Defensible Reports. Your Data Stays Yours.**

Arcana-Forensics develops digital forensics and data protection software for individuals, families, and companies that require rigorous safeguarding of sensitive information. Every module runs locally: no cloud dependencies, no telemetry, and your data never leaves your hardware.

### Why individuals and companies choose Arcana-Forensics
* **Full user control:** 100% offline and air-gap capable. Your data is never uploaded, shared, or exposed to a third-party breach.
* **Enterprise-grade capability:** 200+ artifact parsers and 40+ OSINT modules cover the same ground as large forensic suites.
* **Defensible reporting:** Comprehensive reports in under 5 minutes with strict SHA-256 chain-of-custody logging.
* **Rapid triage:** Device-access auditing, Linux forensics, SMB audits, and password health checks in minutes.
